# 02 — Positioning

## What CoinUp is

- The **Internet’s Bitcoin Arcade**  
- A **living entertainment brand** with games, mascot, lore, merch, events  
- A **skill-and-score floor** where Bitcoin (sats) is the coin  
- Built for **Arch Network** rails  
- Hosted by **Chip** and a growing cast  
- A **forever product surface** — lobby, cabinets, seasons, tournaments  

**One-liner:**  
*Insert Bitcoin. Play. Win Bitcoin. — managed by Chip.*

## What CoinUp isn’t

| Not this | Why |
|----------|-----|
| A casino skin | We don’t lead with house-edge roulette cosplay |
| A generic crypto casino | No “slots but purple” brand |
| Pure GameFi mercenary farming | Play is the product; tokens aren’t the only loop |
| A DeFi protocol with a mascot sticker | TVL is not our hero metric |
| A faceless wallet utility | Culture and cabinets come first |
| “Archade” | Wrong name — product is **CoinUp** |

## Competitive landscape

### Online casino

| | Casino | CoinUp |
|--|--------|--------|
| Core loop | Bet → resolve → repeat | Insert → play skill/score → board/pot |
| Visual | Luxury black/gold or neon greed | Retro arcade + Chip universe |
| Promise | Luck / payout | Play / high score / entertainment |
| Host | None or dealer | Chip + cast |

### Crypto casino

Same as casino, plus token complexity. CoinUp refuses the “another verifiably fair dice” positioning. We may share wallet UX patterns; we do not share soul.

### GameFi

| | GameFi (typical) | CoinUp |
|--|------------------|--------|
| Hero | Token emissions | Cabinets + brand world |
| Risk | Extractive loops | Transparent sat costs, play-led |
| Art | Often generic fantasy | Strict Pixel System + Chip |

### DeFi

DeFi is plumbing. CoinUp may use rails; it does not *market as* yield infrastructure. Never lead homepage with APY.

### Traditional / digital arcades

Closest spiritual kin: **cabinet culture**, tickets, tokens, high scores.  
Difference: Bitcoin unit of account + global networked floor + modern tournament layer.
