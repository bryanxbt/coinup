# 21 — Photography

**Board keywords:** RETRO · VIBRANT · NEON · AUTHENTIC

## Mood

Dark floors, neon signage, real or staged cabinets, gold coin piles, crowd energy, construction Day 1 dirt.

## Do

- Neon rim light (pink/blue)  
- Deep shadows  
- Practical arcade locations or faithful sets  
- Merch on dark seamless or in-world  

## Don't

- Sterile stock office  
- Harsh daylight only with no grade  
- Generic crypto laser hands
