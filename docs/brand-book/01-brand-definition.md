# 01 — Brand Definition

**Source:** Brand Book v1 panels 01–03

## Mission

> **Build the greatest digital arcade ever.**  
> **All games. All people. All the time.**

## Vision

> A world where anyone, anywhere can play fair, fun, competitive games of chance and skill to win Bitcoin.

## Values

| Value | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **FUN** | Play first. Joy is the product. |
| **FAIR** | Clean play. Clear rules. Real scores. |
| **COMMUNITY** | Everyone's welcome on the floor. |
| **FREEDOM** | Bitcoin. Open access. No gatekept fun. |
| **BUILT TO LAST** | Forever floor — not a hype campaign. |

## Brand personality

| Trait | Line |
|-------|------|
| **PLAYFUL** | We don't take ourselves too seriously. |
| **RETRO** | Inspired by the golden age of arcades. |
| **BOLD** | We go big or go home. |
| **INCLUSIVE** | Everyone's welcome at CoinUp. |
| **DEGEN (IN A GOOD WAY)** | We love competition, risk, and high scores. |

## Brand promise

Insert sats → play cabinets → climb boards → win Bitcoin — inside a world hosted by Chip, with craft rules that keep every asset on-universe.

## Audience

Players who want *arcade feel* with Bitcoin; Bitcoiners who want something to *do*; retro culture; streamers & tournament runners; artists shipping into a shared pixel universe.

## Why Bitcoin / Games / Arcades

- **Bitcoin** — the real coin in the machine  
- **Games** — repeatable emotion (insert → run → score → rematch)  
- **Arcades** — place, objects, cabinets, tokens, high scores, physicalizable brand  

## Brand story

Day 1: dirt lot, blueprints, Chip in a varsity jacket. Cabinets arrive. The mission goes on the fence. The floor never closes.
