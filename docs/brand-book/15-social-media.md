# 15 — Social Media

**Style line:** BOLD · FUN · PIXEL PERFECT · COMMUNITY DRIVEN  
**Source:** Brand Book v1 panel 15

## Formats

| Type | Recipe |
|------|--------|
| **New cabinet** | Chip + “NEW CABINET JUST ARRIVED!” + game accent |
| **Tournament** | “KING OF COINUP” + time (e.g. SATURDAY 8PM UTC) |
| **Meme / reaction** | Chip face + caption (“WHEN YOU CASH OUT AT 1.01x”) |

## Voice

- Lowercase OK for tweets; display type on graphics  
- Chip posts = first person floor manager  
- Construction updates = Day 1 honesty  
- No empty GM spam without floor news  

## Templates to build

Announcement · Countdown · Game launch · Leaderboard flex · Reaction 4-pack · Quote card with mission
